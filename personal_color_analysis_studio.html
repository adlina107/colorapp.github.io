How the 12-Season Method Works

1. Undertone (Temperature)

Measures the subtle pigment just under your epidermis. Warm undertones have higher carotene and golden pigments; Cool undertones reflect higher hemoglobin and blue-pink balance; Neutral balances both.

2. Value / Depth (Light vs. Dark)

Derived using Individual Typology Angle ($ITA^\circ = \frac{\arctan(\frac{L^* - 50}{b^*}) \times 180}{\pi}$). This sets the lightness or richness of the optimal palette to prevent skin from appearing shadowy or drained.

3. Chroma (Clear vs. Muted)

High chroma features can support vivid, saturated gemstone hues (Winter / Spring). Soft, muted features look best in dusty, desaturated, greyed-out shades (Summer / Autumn).
